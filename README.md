# Dad Joke Showdown

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5)

## The joke

> My son asked why I put the TV remote in the fridge. I said, 'It needed to chill.'

## Why it works

This is a dad joke in the purest dinner-table sense: a kid catches dad putting the TV remote in the fridge — the classic dad misplacement — and dad answers with a straight face and a pun that was hiding in plain sight. "Chill" is the word for what a fridge does — and it is also what a stressed-out remote supposedly needs. The setup earns the question, "It needed to chill" is the whole punchline, and it is the very last word, so the double meaning lands the instant it is spoken. No setup to explain, no wordplay to untangle, just a father, a fridge, and a groan.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
```
